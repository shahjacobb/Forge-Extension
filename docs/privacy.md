# Privacy policy for Lahza

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Lahza is a focus timer for Chrome. This page is what we do with your data.

## On your computer

By default, everything stays on your device in Chrome’s storage: the timer, session lengths, sound settings, and a history of completed focus and break sessions. That is so the clock and the Activity tab still work after you close the popup.

We do not read your browsing history. We do not look at the pages you have open.

## Optional account

You can create an account with an email and password if you want the same sessions and settings on another Chrome profile. That is optional. The timer works without it.

If you sign up, we send your email, password, display name (if you type one), settings, and completed sessions to our database hosted by [Supabase](https://supabase.com) at `tiopzxojmnortiwbgfrx.supabase.co`. Supabase handles the login. We do not sell that data. We do not use it for ads.

## Notifications and sound

When a session ends, Chrome can show a notification. A short chime can play if you leave sound on. Those stay on your computer.

## What we do not collect

Health data, payment data, location, web history, keystrokes, or the content of websites.

## Sharing

We do not sell your data. The only third party in the picture is Supabase, and only if you create an account, so the sync feature can run.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the extension removes the on-device copy. If you have an account and want that deleted too, open an issue at [github.com/shahjacobb/Forge-Extension/issues](https://github.com/shahjacobb/Forge-Extension/issues) from the email you signed up with.
